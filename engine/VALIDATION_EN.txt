How the changes were found (cell (18, 6) at eps = 1e-3)
-------------------------------------------------------
- v3.3: refusal, the READY peak-time logarithm has no certified positive
  argument. Same refusal with v3.3 + C1 (narrower first auxiliary delay),
  so C1 was dropped.
- v3.3 + C7: the logarithm is bypassed; next refusal "root bracket
  failed" on [0, 0.774]. A single-cell replay logging the bracket tests
  showed: a correct guess (0.2136), the fire root spread over about
  [0.13, 0.34] over the box, the slope positive on every 1/48 piece up to
  0.70 (minimum 0.52), and a negative slope bound only for the single
  evaluation over [0, 0.643]. Hence remark P.
- v3.3 + C7 + P: cell (18, 6) accepted in 329 s; then the full campaign.
