A22 v3.3 — REVIEW GUIDE FOR THE MANUAL LEARNING PROOF
===================================================

What is new
-----------

The current learning theorem supplies the missing universal quantifier over
the stated initial states and fixed physical device parameters. It does not
derive that quantifier from the count of passing cells. Its argument is a
local simulation/coverage invariant, composed through the feedforward graph.
The numerical terminal section and future return theorem are existing inputs.

The proof's most consequential points for independent review are:

1. Pointwise affine soundness and the OPEN IMAGE LEMMA (Sections 4-5).
   Freeze old physical/noise coordinates. A newly evaluated exponential or
   logarithm must leave two-sided latitude around the actual guard value.
   Subsequent arithmetic must preserve that latitude for the selected
   nonzero physical coefficient, even after a hull changes the center.
   The argument is over the projection onto fresh coordinates, not a claim
   of strict center slack at every fixed assignment of those coordinates.

2. Newton ordering and atom freshness (Section 6).
   The derivative is evaluated before the new guard evaluation. Check
   that the selected fresh latitude can vary while the chosen mean slope
   and all old exterior dates stay fixed. Include piecewise derivatives
   across raw cuts and the possible zero rounded-midpoint case. Check
   every retained affine_refine output, not only the final plain bracket.
   This is used to strengthen a weak bound comparison into strict physical
   order for ordinary uncertain roots. Fixed FAST/SLOW dates are separate.

3. Reachable memory joins (Section 9).
   The proof does not assert that equal tokens suffice for arbitrary
   restricted memory anchors. Ordinary/recovery joins use common objects;
   separately recomputed local returns have fresh distinct nonpoint atoms,
   so their memory is dropped. Verify the callers and retained discrete
   fields together with the actual dep.join implementation.

4. All successful event and macro exits (Section 8).
   Check actual-order retention, phase intersections, absence of a hidden
   READY firing at an endpoint, and refusal when optional emission branches
   cannot be represented by the single-train interface. The commutation
   argument specializes to the complete recorded list of 202 uses.
   Review both ordinary root construction and _first_crossing_cover:
   Section 8(vi) now proves the latter by left-to-right slice coverage,
   continuity and endpoint signs, without a derivative assumption. The
   saved records do not identify which construction served those uses.
   There is no claim that a zero count for the new recovery-group diagnostic
   excludes uses of the older single-event local-cycle fallback.

5. Artificial group clocks (companion GROUP_ENTRY_CLOCK_LEMMA_EN.txt).
   Check that the inclusive FAST-section overlap test runs before ordinary
   advance, and that the protected macro checks its entry ordering. Those
   facts close the case where a single-event group is later extended in
   SLOW. Duration clipping alone would not close this obligation.

6. Global witnesses and theorem scope (Sections 10-11).
   Shared parents must retain the same physical date at all destinations.
   Fresh local abstractions must extend that assignment without resetting
   it. Distinguish fixed influential gains at future entry from masked
   gains permitted to evolve, and distinguish the quiescent section Q
   from its between-section trajectory tube.

Evidence versus assumptions
---------------------------

The supplied source witness checker verifies 110 structural/hash facts
with 40 function records. The previous audits authenticate 216 payloads,
lineage and receipts and 63,888 distinct retained time atoms with no
conflicting definition. Two earlier independent arithmetic checkers support
the future inequalities. None of these is a proof-assistant verification
of the new local simulation argument, a complete transition trace, or an
independent qualification of the native binary.

Assumptions A1-A4 explicitly retain authentic execution, the arithmetic and
language contracts, fail-closed assertions/exceptions, and namespace/context
discipline. The source review checks the corresponding runner mechanisms.
It does not replace the need to trust the runtime/toolchain at this level
of a computation-assisted mathematical proof.

Corrections to earlier descriptions
-----------------------------------

* Effective D1_PORTS are distinct per-port affine symbols enclosing the
  first-delay interval; they are not plain occurrences. Later D0 keeps
  the fixed port parameter dependence.
* Effective U0 is the point upper(2*delta/(LE*RM)), not the logarithmic
  exact terminal-ERROR duration. The latter is bounded by this slab.
* The full relative_order_proofs list is exported; truncation applies to
  the twelve largest-width diagnostics instead.
* The runner rejects optimized Python with an explicit if not __debug__
  exception, rather than relying on an assert that -O would remove.

No new campaign, cell replay or modification of the supplied program was
performed. The archived experimental_not_qualified status is preserved.
This dossier's conclusion is the supplied manual proof under its stated
assumptions. Independent expert acceptance and formal verification are
