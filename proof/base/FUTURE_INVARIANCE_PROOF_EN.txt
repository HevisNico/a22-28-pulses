A22 v3.3 — A COMPUTER-ASSISTED FUTURE-INVARIANCE ARGUMENT

Scope and theorem

This document proves a future-return property for a concrete quiescent domain
Q(theta) of the declared hybrid model. The finite numerical inequalities are
checked by two implementations with different arithmetic and transcendental
series strategies. This is a computer-assisted mathematical argument, not a
formal verification in a proof assistant.

Applying this theorem to EVERY trajectory of the training family additionally
requires the training-entry statement H_entry:

  For every permitted initial state and fixed per-port parameter realization,
  the physical state at T=1116 belongs to Q(theta).

The supplied training artifacts give exact candidate enclosures and consistent
provenance. They do not by themselves prove H_entry. The conclusion about the
entire training family is therefore explicitly conditional on H_entry.

THEOREM. Fix any admissible theta and x in Q(theta). Apply one canonical source
pulse on lane 0 at time s, and no other source pulse during the following 20
units. Then:

  (i) each of 191 active cells fires exactly once, within its certified phase
      interval relative to s; the other 25 cells never fire;
 (ii) all currents and auxiliary cycles end and every main cell is READY
      before s+12.791;
(iii) the resulting quiescent state belongs to Q(theta), and remains there
      during arbitrary additional idle time.

Consequently the same result holds for every finite or infinite source word
whose successive gaps are at least 20. With H_entry it applies to all allowed
training trajectories followed by every such future source word.

A. Definition of Q(theta)

The file invariant_section_domain.json defines the numerical bounds, indexed
by layer, lane and port. They come from all_terminal_exact.json; the phase and
port classifications come from future_closure_certificate.json.

Theta fixes, independently for every physical port, C_i, LM_i, LS_i, LE_i and
M_i within the relative +/-1e-4 parameter ranges of the supplied model. The
canonical pulse exponent a=ln(4), kappa=1/2, r=1/10 and r_m=1/40 are fixed.

Every principal and auxiliary mode is READY. There is no active incoming,
outgoing or delayed physical current and no unprocessed physical event.
For the 191 active cells q=r exactly. The remaining 25 main charges lie in
their extracted intervals, each containing r and lying below threshold 1.
For each of the 540 active incoming ports, m_i=r_m exactly. Each of the 108
silent ports has its extracted auxiliary interval containing r_m below 1.

All reserves satisfy 50<=E<=100. For each cell, b lies between its individually
rounded-down terminal lower bound and 1.1. These lower bounds are positive.

There are three gain classes:
- 525 influential active ports: the extracted gain interval;
- 15 masked active ports: [terminal gain lower bound, C_i(theta)];
- 108 silent ports: the extracted gain interval.
Every interval is intersected with the physical constraint 0<g_i<=C_i(theta).
Influential and silent gains will remain fixed. Masked gains may increase to
their physical caps. The domain does not require b to remain equal to its
training value; b may increase to 1.1.

The construction deliberately retains the full physical state. Historical
integration records are implementation representations, not additional forces.
No claim is made that a particular engine history can simply be deleted or
reinitialized without a separate representation theorem.

B. Uniform resource and auxiliary bounds

The main/auxiliary phase domains give nonnegative power expenditure. A uniform
upper bound, with all parameter maxima taken outward, is

 W <= (3 Cmax + 3 Mmax + 1) a + 1.1 + 4.5 LMmax + 2.4 LEmax
   < 240.567 < 500.

The bound uses LMmax as an upper bound for all auxiliary drain coefficients;
the independent checker also verifies LSmax<LMmax and LEmax<LMmax. The 4.5
coefficient covers three auxiliary drains and up to three half-strength mark
actuators. The 2.4 coefficient covers three error actuators of size at most
8*(1/10)*LEmax. Admission and fast-mode terms give the remaining terms.

At E=50 the derivative 10(100-E)-W is positive, and at E=100 it is nonpositive.
Thus [50,100] is invariant and power gating never shuts off an actuator in
this domain. Main and auxiliary charges remain within their phase sections;
gain and b caps are projected as prescribed by the model.

Write

 h(u)=a/(a-kappa) [exp(-kappa*u)-exp(-a*u)].

The auxiliary first threshold from r_m occurs at D0, the unique root of
r_m+M_i*h(u)=1 in (0,0.1). The certificate brackets this root uniformly over
M_i. Later phase durations are exactly

 DM=.02/(1+eta_m), DS=1.06/(1+eta_s), DE=.15/(1+eta_e),

where each eta is independently in [-1e-4,1e-4]. Let
E_start=D0+DM+DS and E_end=E_start+DE. Certified bounds give E_start>1 and
E_end<1.3. All first future inputs on active ports start from r_m, so D0, not
the special initial-slab D1 delay, is the relevant future delay.

C. Certified head response, by induction on layers

Set the source phase interval to [0,0] on lane 0 and leave other source lanes
inactive. Each next cell receives only the certified active parent pulses,
whose arrival phases have intervals [L_i,U_i]. For a READY cell at q=r,
before the first input pulse ends and before any error actuation starts,

 q(t)=r+sum_i g_i*h_+(t-t_i),

where h_+(u)=0 for u<=0 and h_+(u)=h(u) for 0<u<1.

The checker proves a*exp(-a)>kappa*exp(-kappa); hence h is strictly increasing
on (0,1]. Using early arrivals and upper gains gives an upper guard, while
late arrivals and lower gains give a lower guard. For every active cell it
certifies a phase interval [F_minus,F_plus] satisfying

 upper_guard(F_minus)<1,     lower_guard(F_plus)>1.

It also verifies F_plus<min_i L_i+1. Monotonicity bounds the entire pre-root
interval, not just sampled points: before F_minus, q is below threshold; by
F_plus the threshold has been crossed, with a positive derivative. This
isolates the first upward crossing for every admissible realization.

Every incoming port with L_i>F_plus is classified as masked. Its pulse has
not yet started at the crossing. The two strict guards are checked again
with masked ports omitted completely. Therefore their bounds do not depend
on a masked gain retaining its training value: any admissible future masked
gain up to C_i has no effect on this firing time.

The remaining ports are influential. The minimum strict gap between the
latest firing bound and the earliest possible error actuation is greater
than 0.183717. Thus no influential gain changes before this first crossing.
All 191 active cells pass these checks; their output intervals supply the
next layer's arrival intervals. The inactive cells have no active incoming
port, and their initial subthreshold charges relax toward r without firing.

D. Protection, gain preservation and exactly one firing

FAST lasts one unit and ends at q=1/4. During SLOW, q'=-b(t)q with b positive
and nondecreasing up to 1.1. If beta is the cell's domain lower bound for b,
then every protection duration P satisfies

 1+ln(5/2)/1.1 <= P <= 1+ln(5/2)/beta.

Denote these uniform bounds Pmin and Pmax. The certificate checks

 F_minus+Pmin > U_i+E_end  for every influential input port,
 F_minus+Pmin > U_i+1     for every active input port.

The first margin exceeds 0.596642 uniformly. Together with the pre-error
firing inequality, it places every influential ERROR phase entirely under
principal protection. Its gain therefore remains fixed. The second margin
exceeds 0.100143 uniformly: every raw input pulse has ended before principal
reopening, including every masked pulse.

Masked gains may be actuated after reopening. They remain between their
entry lower bound and C_i by monotonicity and projection, exactly as allowed
in Q(theta). Silent gains receive no auxiliary event and remain fixed.
Recovery b also stays within [beta,1.1] by monotonicity and projection.

At reopening, q=r. Since all raw inputs have ended, q remains r, so there
cannot be a second firing from the same source pulse. Auxiliary cycles end
before a later pulse can arrive. All relevant strict margins are positive;
none is a numerical equality accepted by tolerance.

E. Simultaneous events

Arrivals within a cohort need not have a fixed order. The head formula adds
all raw contributions and is valid with equal arrival times; h_+(0)=0 gives
charge continuity at arrival. No priority is imposed by sorting interval
midpoints. An arrival simultaneous with the principal firing cannot change
q instantaneously; after firing, protection closes admission in every
zero-duration ordering consistent with the declared first-crossing semantics.

The strict raw-end, error and reopening margins exclude the dangerous
head/end or reopening/raw-current boundary cases used in this proof.
Auxiliary events on distinct ports act on distinct auxiliary coordinates;
their mark effects add in the continuous scalar b equation. Gain and b stops
are monotone projections. Reordering simultaneous switches for zero elapsed
time does not add an impulse or alter these continuous states. A masked
error may overlap reopening, but its gain is allowed to evolve and remains
irrelevant to the certified head response. These observations apply to the
declared weak-order hybrid semantics, not to an arbitrary alternative rule
with impulsive jumps or priority-dependent physical resets.

F. Global return and infinite continuation

For each cell the checker bounds the latest main reopening, the end of every
incoming auxiliary cycle, and the end of its outgoing pulse. The maximum
across the whole network is bounded above by

 12.790360113606780 < 12.791.

Thus the WHOLE network, not merely one cell, is quiescent before 12.791 after
each source pulse. There is more than 7.209 units of global idle margin before
the earliest allowed next source pulse at 20. No overlapping-wave assumption
is needed for this certified domain.

The physical state then lies in Q(theta): gains and b satisfy the preserved
bounds, active main/auxiliary charges are at their reset rest values, silent
charges lie in intervals containing their relaxation equilibria, no current
remains active, and E stays in [50,100]. Additional idle time preserves Q.
Hence the one-pulse return map P_h satisfies

 P_h(Q(theta)) subset Q(theta) for every h>=20.

Induction proves the claim for any finite sequence of pulses. Infinite
sequences with gaps>=20 cannot accumulate in finite time; each wave has only
finitely many events in this finite feedforward graph. This proves the same
claim for all admissible infinite source words.

Q is a quiescent SECTION domain. During a pulse the state naturally leaves
that section. For continuous-time invariance, augment Q with the certified
one-pulse prefixes for ages 0 through 20 and an external source-age clock,
allowing the next pulse only when its age is at least 20. Return to Q before
12.791 and preservation during idle time prove forward invariance of this
full hybrid tube. This explicitly includes modes, active pulses and clocks,
rather than claiming that a gain box alone is a continuous-time invariant.

G. Numerical certification and trusted basis

certify_future.py uses directed integer interval arithmetic on the grid
10^-90. It bounds exponentials by range reduction, a degree-45 Taylor series,
a geometric tail bound and interval squaring. Logarithms use 220 terms of
the positive atanh series with an explicit tail. Root bisection constructs
brackets, which are accepted only after strict endpoint guard checks.

verify_future_certificate.py performs no root search. It independently reads
the fixed brackets and rechecks all head guards, parameter delays, protection
inequalities and the global return bound. It uses 70-digit directed Decimal
basic arithmetic, an unscaled degree-90 exponential series and 250-term
logarithm series, again with explicit tails. It does not call library exp or
ln and does not import the first arithmetic implementation or the A22 engine.

Both implementations pass. The remaining trusted basis includes the stated
physical equations, the mathematical argument above, Python integer/Decimal
basic arithmetic, the two small checker implementations, and the extraction
of exact endpoint data. This is not a formal verification of these programs.
In particular, agreement between these checkers does not certify the A22
training integrator or its branch coverage.

H. The remaining entry obligation

The audit verifies all 216 payloads, exact state subviews, receipt hashes,
parent lineage and the supplied program identity. It establishes that the
numbers used here are the supplied campaign's numbers. It cannot infer from
those hashes that the physical training reachable set lies within them.

To conclude an unconditional result for the original training family, a
soundness/composition argument is still required for the actually exercised
training paths: initial-slab coverage; independent-port parameter routing;
outward arithmetic and root isolation; complete weak-order coverage; valid
joins and history aggregation; partial mark/error integration; commutation
logic; and feedforward composition. The 202 fire-arrival commutation records
are concentrated in layer 18 lanes 4 and 8. The no-fire cover and the new
recovery-group fallback were not used anywhere in the supplied campaign.

The four H1/H2 reports and the recorded tests support parts of this review;
they are not a substitute for that soundness theorem. A prior written local
continuation proof, if available, must be checked against this exact v3.3
implementation and its per-port changes before it is used to discharge
H_entry. This document does not silently assume that earlier shared-port
proofs apply unchanged.
