1. Statement and scope
----------------------

Let Theta be the fixed-device parameter family of full_model_reference.md:
independent per-cell/per-port physical constants in their prescribed relative

denote the physical hybrid trajectory, including its emitted pulse history.

THEOREM (learning coverage). Under assumptions A1-A4 below, every admissible
learning trajectory is covered by the recorded computation. In particular,

  for every theta and admissible x0, X_theta,x0(1116) belongs to S_18,

and every physical training emission is represented in the corresponding

  H_entry: Reach_training(1116) subset Q.

The theorem is specific to the stated training protocol and physical family.
It is not a statement about arbitrary input waveforms or arbitrary initial

contain that date. Labels alone are not identities.

I3. At least one retained actuation/deposit ledger for the physical gated

I4. If READY memory exists, its anchor, anchor charge, admitted amplitudes

the scalar state still encloses the physical charge.
I5. The remaining exterior queue contains every unprocessed action; the

One harmless boundary representation is allowed: FAST at exact age 1 may

4. Arithmetic inclusion and a new openness argument
---------------------------------------------------

An affine form denotes [c-,c+]+sum a_i*eta_i+[-e,e]. At fixed existing eta,
addition encloses center sums, coefficient sums and their rounding errors.
Multiplication follows by expanding the product around floating center
representatives. The remainder bound is

 dx*Ry + dy*Rx + Ax*ey + Ay*ex + (Rx+ex)*(Ry+ey) + coefficient_error,

where Rx,Ry bound coefficient radii, dx,dy bound center deviations and Ax,Ay
bound center magnitudes. The source computes these nonnegative terms with
outward bound operations. Unary operations use the mean-value identity
about a center point, bounding slope deviation and input uncertainty.
Reciprocal refuses an interval containing zero. Truncation adds discarded
coefficient mass to the remainder. Promotion represents that remainder by
a fresh coordinate. Neither removes a represented value.

For a union choose any common coefficient vector a*. Branch i is covered
by shifting its center by a radius at least e_i+sum|a_i-a*|. The implemented
hull is therefore pointwise. The lattice helpers retain an operand only
under whole-range dominance; otherwise the plain min/max interval covers
the pointwise operation. Domain intersections are used only where the
physical invariant or a second valid enclosure supplies their premise.

These arguments prove the transfer contracts for arbitrary represented
operand values, not just for values coming from physical trajectories.
That stronger domain of validity yields the following useful lemma.

OPEN IMAGE LEMMA. Freeze the pre-existing coordinates and all other operands.
Suppose an intermediate form can represent an open interval about its actual
value using only its unlabelled center/remainder freedom and coordinates
allocated during this calculation. Suppose its remaining use is affine in
that value with a nonzero actual coefficient, or is continuous and strictly
monotone on a neighbourhood of that value. Sound arithmetic represents
an open interval about the final actual value, while all old data stay fixed.

Proof. Perturb the intermediate value slightly in both directions, within
its represented interval. Keeping the other actual operand values fixed,
the exact remaining expression changes to both sides of its actual value.
Sound transfer operations contain both sides and everything
between them. Error promotion may require different assignments to fresh
coordinates for different perturbations; this is permitted. Truncation
restores lost dependence as remainder freedom. Neither contracts the set
of representable final values. For a hull, its pointwise union contains
the perturbed selected constituent. End of proof.

This is a projection statement over newly introduced coordinates. It does
NOT assert an open center interval at one fixed assignment of all those
new coordinates. That unnecessarily strong requirement caused the earlier
strict-center route to stall, especially after a hull changed the center.
The proof never varies an old physical parameter or an old external date.
Freshness and the indicated expression use are essential.

5. Instantiation at the actual guards
-------------------------------------

5.1 Scalar READY guard.
At a positive elapsed probe u the scalar expression is

 R+(q0-R)*exp(-K*u)+J*(exp(-K*u)-exp(-A*u))/(A-K)-1.

If an ascending root exists from q0<1, J>0. The second exponential is a
separate evaluated operand and appears with coefficient -J/(A-K), which
is nonzero. Its expanded scalar exponential, or the expanded center of
its affine unary evaluation, strictly encloses its exact value. The exact
exp(0)=1 shortcut does not apply to a positive actual A*u when the argument
is a plain exact zero; symbolic evaluation expands its center even at zero.
The open image lemma supplies an open guard enclosure at positive probes.
At elapsed zero the live-state fact q0<1 directly excludes a root.

5.2 READY pulse memory.
An increasing root from a below-threshold READY anchor requires a nonzero
admitted pulse contribution. All pulse origins already stored at a live
control point are at or before that point. Thus at a positive elapsed
probe, at least one contributing pulse or nonzero raw tail has positive
admitted age. Its amplitude is positive and constant during its own raw
pulse, as justified in Section 7.

For h(u) on 0<=u<=1, the symbolic branch uses the difference of two
exponentials and a nonzero positive multiplier A/(A-K). The selected
second exponential supplies the open operand just as above. The plain
branch uses monotone endpoint bounds. At a positive actual age, its
lower endpoint is strictly below the response: either the lower age is
zero and the response is positive, or that endpoint exponential bound is
strict. Its upper endpoint is strictly above the response. Thus the plain
returned interval is open about that actual response.

After raw end, the response h(1)*exp(-K*(age-1)) has a strictly enclosed
positive h(1); its exponential multiplier is nonzero. For an age interval
straddling 1, the lower bound is the minimum of the two endpoint bounds
and the upper bound is h(1).hi, strictly above the exact peak. A positive
actual response is strictly between them, including age exactly 1.

For a tail, initial_age is physically in [0,1], admitted=min(elapsed,1-age)
and after=max(0,elapsed-(1-age)). The lattice/domain operations occur BEFORE
the response h(admitted) is evaluated. Choose a term whose actual admitted
duration is positive. The resulting h operand is open as just proved and
is subsequently multiplied only by its positive gain and positive decay
factors. No clipping follows that response. Terms with zero contribution
need supply no slack. Summing the memory therefore retains an open guard
enclosure. This includes hull-enclosed gains; their center need not be
positive, since the open image lemma uses the actual nonzero multiplier.

5.3 SLOW and per-order return guards.
ell=ln(2.5)-capped_dose. The logarithm is evaluated with adjacent-number
expansion, not an exact ln(1) shortcut. Holding the dose fixed, its use has
coefficient +1, or -1 for -ell. Each selected history consequently has an
open guard enclosure; the history union retains it. Live probes use already
processed mark-end information only after those ends. Return probes starting
at F+1 use live=False, so a probe before a recorded mark end still uses the
unfinished primitive. This is explicit in return_with_marks.

5.4 Auxiliary roots and first-delay comparisons.
Their guards have the same positive M*h(u) structure, with initial charge
strictly below 1. They use the same open exponential response. The later
per-port D0 is fixed for a fixed physical M and may be reused at every pulse.
The first-delay D1 is an outer abstraction of the allowed initial transient.

Correction to an earlier source description: D1_PORTS in the effective
port_box_adapter are distinct affine symbols about the plain D1 midpoint,
one per port. They are not plain occurrences. Each is used for that port's
first input only, and its radius outwardly covers D1, so the abstraction
still covers every physical first delay without equating different ports.

6. Root refinement, strict ordering, and fresh return identities
---------------------------------------------------------------

Fix an actual root. An accepted bracket has f(l).hi<=0, f(h).lo>=0 and a
strictly positive derivative bound. If a positive endpoint were the actual
root, Section 5's open guard enclosure would extend to both sides of zero,
contradicting the corresponding accepted sign. At elapsed zero the initial
mode invariant supplies strict sign. Therefore l<r<h.

At every Newton step, choose a mean slope s>0 such that r=m-f(m)/s.
For a smooth segment this is f'(xi); across raw cuts use the integral
average of the piecewise derivative, which lies in its enclosing interval.
When r=m, any positive enclosed slope gives the identity. The source
evaluates S=deriv(T) BEFORE fun(I(m)). Freeze the
representation of s in S. The subsequent guard evaluation supplies fresh
open freedom, independent of that fixed S. Division by s and subtraction
from m preserve an open interval about r by the open image lemma. Passing
to plain bounds and intersecting with the previous strict bracket keeps r
strictly inside. Induction proves noncollapse for all finite contractions.

If rounding of an extremely small nonnegative midpoint produces m=0,
the live-state guard f(0) is strictly negative. Instead of varying f(0),
vary the enclosed mean slope in S while holding f(0) fixed. At positive
probes the scalar derivative has a separate exponential with nonzero
coefficient A*J/(A-K). Memory derivatives use expanded pulse derivatives,
positive raw-current exponentials, or nonzero decaying tails. Their
pointwise enclosures and convex projection over new coordinates enclose
an open interval about the positive mean slope, including an average
across cuts. The selected derivative exponential is not reused by the
subsequent guard evaluation. Since -f(0)/s is strictly monotone for
positive s and f(0)<0, the Newton image is open here as well. SLOW may
instead use its open logarithmic guard even at zero. No assumption that
a rounded midpoint is strictly positive is necessary.

affine_refine returns either such a correlated Newton image or a previously
retained image/plain bracket. Every choice encloses an open neighbourhood
of r with old inputs fixed. This remains true if a correlated image is
slightly wider than the previous bracket. The mean-value identity, not
subset containment, justifies its root inclusion.

The scalar ready_root first bounds the root by the upper and lower scalar
comparison solutions. Monotonicity in q0 and J orders their first roots.
Strict outward endpoints for the comparison roots make the combined plain
bracket strict, including a physical realization at either parameter
extreme. Its subsequent correlated refinement is covered by the same
argument. Failure of an extremal root call causes refusal, not omission.

Every ordinary accepted root duration is consequently nonpoint. Its final
shift allocates a new time atom, absent from the older exterior queue and
control state. For a small abstract perturbation of the root duration,
the shifted base and the time expression can both be represented using
their fresh arithmetic witnesses with all old data fixed. Subtracting an
old exterior date therefore has values on both sides of the actual date
difference. The new duration atom does not cancel in the expression path.
The absolute-box subtraction and the expression subtraction each strictly
enclose that difference; choosing the narrower remains strict.

Hence a weak accepted lower-bound comparison >=0 against an ordinary root
actually proves strict physical ordering. A true simultaneous READY fire
or nonconstant SLOW return cannot pass that ordinary ordering branch.
Strict counterfactual-charge/log-charge ordering proofs already have the
same conclusion by continuity and first-root uniqueness. Constant recovery
uses explicit strict comparisons and otherwise refuses. Unresolved cases
must pass a covering macro or prevent a complete result.

For FAST/SLOW, exact equality is explicitly normalized by the causal wrapper.
If another weak-boundary representation processes an exterior action first
at F+1, the pending fixed section remains in fire+1, both modes have identical
gates, and the next advance/macro normalizes it without positive elapsed
time. Thus no observable event or actuator transfer is lost. This is the
specific boundary exception in I2, not a general priority convention.

Distinct calls to return_with_marks create distinct nonpoint-duration atoms.
Restricting a return's absolute box in the local solver preserves its
expression and therefore preserves that distinct atom. It cannot turn two
separately recomputed returns into the same memory anchor token.

The initialization slab endpoint in effective settings is the POINT

charges remain below threshold on this slab by the previously checked

 R+[-delta,delta]*exp(-K*U0)+[0,3*Cmax*A*U0].

Each initial ERROR deposits at most 8*delta in its log-gain, so the stored
initial offset [-delta,9*delta] covers it. Initial b remains .08±delta.
Active source tails at U0 are retained. Fast/slow auxiliary comparison
roots cover D1; later roots cover D0. The independent initial certificate
provides additional finite checks of these uniform inequalities.

Every auxiliary cycle ends before age 1.3; ERROR starts after its own raw
end. Main interfire time exceeds 1+ln(2.5)/1.1 >1.3, and source gaps are 8.
Thus every subsequent input on a port sees auxiliary rest, and each port's
gain is constant during its own raw pulse. The initially overlapping ERROR
is entirely absorbed into the initial slab bound before this argument is
used. Distinct fixed port constants retain distinct physical choices.

Variation of constants gives the scalar READY formula in Section 5. For
memory, each new pulse contributes g*h(age) until age 1 and then its
homogeneous tail. An already-active raw pulse at reopening contributes

 g*exp(-A*v)*h(min(u,1-v))*exp(-K*max(u-(1-v),0)),

with physical v in [0,1]. Its derivative is -K times that response plus
the actual admitted current. These are the implemented memory expressions.
Without a new arrival, the derivative cannot change from negative to
positive: between cuts d'=-K*d-A*J, and cuts decrease d. Thus an ascending
threshold root is the first crossing. SLOW has ell'=-b<0 and a unique return.

Actuator histories integrate charge transfers exactly before outward
enclosure. A full MARK gives .1 to b; a full ERROR gives .6 to z. Partial
gates retain their origins and endpoints. The nonnegative potential is

force saturation for a larger physical cap. Partial MARKs intercepted at
firing end within DM<1, hence before F+1; their final transfer correctly
acts throughout SLOW. New marks use their time-dependent primitive.

For a cap-contact bracket [l,u], the uncapped integral to y0 followed by
the cap exceeds the true dose by a nonnegative amount at most
(u-l)*max(|raw_rate(l)-B|,|raw_rate(u)-B|). The optimized evaluator subtracts
[0,error] only when contact is certain before the earliest endpoint.
Otherwise monotone quadrature encloses the contact strip. Intersections
with 0, B*duration and the uncapped dose are physical upper/lower bounds.

Completed-history folding replaces only additive completed contributions.
After folding, identical remaining fire/mark or active-deposit structure
can hull its baseline pointwise. Active endpoints remain explicit. Keeping
the ledger unions preserves the selected physical branch; removing unused
condition labels enlarges the set. Budget exhaustion fails closed.

The uniform work bound is <240.566579<500. At E=50 the pump is 500 and
dominates consumption; at E=100 its derivative is nonpositive. This proves
E in [50,100] and justifies the continuously open power gates. It uses the
physical charge/cap phase bounds above, not sampled energy trajectories.

8. Event coverage and all successful macro exits
------------------------------------------------

For each physical exterior weak order, admissible_orders retains a path:
the next physical event cannot violate a certified strict time contradiction,
an absolute-order bound, or its actual port automaton stage. Equality is
not pruned. The suffix minimum examines every unprocessed lower bound, so
an overlap group cannot omit an event strictly inside its common endpoint.
Events exactly at an endpoint may be consumed with zero elapsed time.

Artificial lower group endpoints require an additional forward-entry
argument; nonnegative duration clipping alone is not that argument.
GROUP_ENTRY_CLOCK_LEMMA_EN.txt supplies the complete control-path split.
In particular, FAST-section absolute overlap is intercepted by the protected
macro BEFORE ordinary advance. That macro checks group-lower >= control-upper.
Ordinary FAST-to-SLOW normalization while approaching a single event can
therefore leave SLOW only with its section box wholly before the original
group minimum. Recovery extension cannot decrease that minimum. Combined
with consumed-block queue separation and internal steps certified before
an existing point L, this proves every successful live group entry is
physically forward. No backward SLOW staging convention is needed.

(i) Ordinary advance is sound by the scalar/memory formulas, first-root
argument and Section 6. If a maximum is strictly below 1 there is no fire.
If J=0, a below-threshold charge relaxes toward R<1. A strict no-fire cover
proves the same fact on every closed piece. Every other unresolved guard
causes a covering macro or a refusal.

(ii) Ordinary exterior groups follow all retained orders and require
guardfree flow on each subsegment. The actual order survives and reaches
the common endpoint with its original emission prefix. Joining q and the
histories covers it; compatible modes/raw origins are checked.

(iii) Protected FAST/SLOW groups use the same closed READY/gain gates and
open protected-MARK gate on both sides of F+1. Accumulate all marks in the
actual exterior order. If a first return occurred before the endpoint, the
continued-protection ell would be zero there and strictly decrease after
it, contradicting the accepted endpoint ell.lo>0. Therefore no return
occurred. Normalizing the endpoint to SLOW q=R*exp(ell) is valid even though
the temporary FAST q used during the macro was not a physical SLOW charge.

(iv) Recovery/auxiliary groups predict a return independent of the included
events; the caller excludes raw changes and new effective marks. Insert
that one return among all weak exterior orders. Reopening sets q=R and
opens the correct ERROR gates. Subsequent guardfree propagation and q.hi<1
exclude a new fire. Coincident finite currents have zero integral over a
zero-duration gate interval, so both weak orders cover equality.

(v) The local-cycle resolver recomputes the return using each order's marks.
It prunes only strict contradictions or empty physical phase intersections.
Its window is narrower than 1 after the earliest return; at most one new
fire can occur and its FAST phase covers the remaining window. The installed
READY helper uses an elapsed-time root cover with the original anchor.
The true root cannot be excluded by a slab whose whole charge excludes 1.
The complementary no-fire endpoint lies in [0,1]. An actual new fire has
a retained emission branch. The single-train caller joins only a nonempty
forest with NO new emissions and EVERY branch READY; otherwise it refuses.
Thus a successful zero-emission join cannot hide a physical threshold at
its endpoint. The grouped-recovery fallback uses the identical rule.

(vi) All 202 successful recorded fire/exterior commutations have one
mark_off, two orders and zero fire-time widening. That action does not
change READY charge, so both orders share the same first fire. If the
mark ends before firing, none remains. If firing comes first, its remaining
transfer is retained under protection; at equality the transfer is zero.
The join is after both events and before F+1 and the next pending event.
One common fire and no READY memory cover every realization. The full
relative_order_proofs list is exported without the truncation used for the
twelve largest-width diagnostics, so this specialization is supported by
the complete recorded use list. A general claim for unobserved raw-arrival
commutations is not needed for this campaign theorem.

This conclusion covers BOTH ways _fire_root constructs its first-fire
enclosure. If the ordinary root search raises an accepted exception, it
calls _first_crossing_cover. The caller has already certified f(hi).lo>=0,
and the cover requires f(0).hi<0, where f(u)=Q(t0+u)-1. For each parameter
realization, continuity therefore gives a first threshold time in [0,hi].
The cover scans closed slices from left to right. A slice with f.hi<0
contains no threshold time and is skipped; the left endpoint of the first
undecided slice is retained as the lower bound. A slice with f.lo>=0
certifies a crossing no later than its left endpoint and supplies an upper
bound; absent such a slice, hi remains the certified upper bound. Thus the
returned interval encloses every realization's FIRST threshold time, even
with tangency or nonmonotone charge, without any slope assumption. In the
recorded mark_off-only commutations this encloses the same physical first
fire in both orders, which is all the macro argument above requires; the
ordinary-root strict-interior lemma is not needed for this fallback.
The saved commutation records do not distinguish the two construction
paths, so no assertion is made that the fallback was unused in the 202
successful commutations. The upper endpoint condition is Q>=1, not Q>1.

Spurious prefixes or extended virtual states may fail an assertion and
abort the whole cell; this cannot delete an actual path while still
producing a complete result. Optional emissions cannot become a single
unconditional train through a successful local join.

9. Memory anchors and retained discrete fields
-----------------------------------------------

dep.join keeps the first anchor/origin objects and hulls charges/amplitudes.
The required condition is stronger than equal tokens: those retained objects
must cover the common physical dates for every joined branch. At every
reachable successful caller this follows as follows.

Ordinary exterior joins inherit one incoming memory anchor. Added origins
come from the immutable common queue. Recovery/auxiliary joins create their
memories with one common predicted-return object and the same active raw
origins. Protected and recorded fire-commutation joins have memory=None.

Local-cycle branches recompute returns separately. By Section 6 each
nonpoint duration carries a distinct fresh atom, retained by absolute
restriction. Different return anchors therefore fail dep.join's token test
and memory is dropped. For one branch its own memory is kept. The same
argument covers the joint-return helper if used. Dropping memory removes
correlation but does not remove the scalar charge or any actual path.

This excludes the earlier same-token/different-restricted-box countermodel
from these call sites. It does not claim dep.join is sound on arbitrary
invented memories. Active raw/MARK/ERROR origins are also common: per-port
cycles do not overlap and the common processed queue prefix determines the
unique active cycle. Stale inactive origin fields have no physical effect.
At local zero-emission joins the incoming main fire identity remains common;
at fire commutations the new fire object is explicitly common. Thus the
first branch's retained discrete fields do not identify different live
physical events.

10. Induction to the learning terminal section
----------------------------------------------

The initial-slab argument establishes I1-I5 for every physical realization.
Sections 4-9 show that every successful ordinary or macro successor retains
a covering path and extends its noise witness without changing old physical
choices. Every accepted emission is a physical first crossing for that
realization; every physical emission is either processed or appears as an
optional branch that would prevent a zero-emission success. Finite counters
and budgets cannot produce a success by truncating unfinished work.

Induction over a completed cell loop yields its complete physical output
history and terminal state. The terminal checks require READY main and
auxiliaries and no active raw input. The end event is at 1116. Queue
construction, ordering and consumption account for every earlier exterior
action. A terminal ambiguity cannot be converted into a successful receipt.

For layer 1 the source dates are exactly 8k. Assume the complete common
parent histories have covering global witnesses through layer n-1. A cell
in layer n loads those same parent definitions, extends only its own fresh
namespace and applies the local result. Its witness extension cannot alter
the parent dates already used by another child. Proceed cell by cell and
layer by layer. Acyclic feedforward composition establishes joint coverage
of all 216 terminal states, not merely independently resampled marginals.

The passive audit ties this argument to the recorded run: all 216 complete
typed payloads, their receipts and parent lineage were authenticated; the
87 source files agree with the supplied source archive and the recorded
program snapshots. The retained clock audit found no conflicting definition
among 63,888 distinct atoms. Those facts bind the proof target and data;
they are not being used as substitutes for the local argument above.

The terminal extraction and physical caps place the resulting state in Q.
For quiescent auxiliary ports that have fired, m=RM exactly; silent ports
retain their certified relaxation intervals. Influential gains use their
terminal enclosures; potentially plastic masked gains use the certified
extension to their physical caps. Thus H_entry follows under A1-A4.
