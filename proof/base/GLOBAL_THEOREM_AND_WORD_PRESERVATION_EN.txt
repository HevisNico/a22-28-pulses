The unconditional result proved for the certified section

Fix any admissible physical parameter vector theta. Take any physical state x
in the quiescent domain Q(theta) specified by invariant_section_domain.json.
Let s_0,s_1,... be any finite or infinite sequence of canonical source-pulse
times on lane 0, with s_0 at or after the section and s_(k+1)-s_k >= 20.
All statements below concern the given ideal hybrid equations and weak-order
semantics, not arbitrary input currents or additional hidden pulses.

For every such x, theta and source sequence:

1. Each of the 191 active cells fires exactly once per source pulse. The 25
   inactive cells never fire.
2. The whole network becomes quiescent before s_k+12.791, returns to Q(theta),
   and stays in Q during the remaining idle interval.
3. There is a fixed latency d_(n,j), for each active cell, such that its kth
   future firing is exactly F_(n,j),k = s_k + d_(n,j).
4. Consequently every active output preserves the temporal word exactly:

     F_(n,j),k+1 - F_(n,j),k = s_(k+1) - s_k.

The latencies depend on theta AND the influential gains in the chosen entry
state x. They need not be the same for different admissible entry states or
for different lanes. This theorem does not assert a universal propagation
speed, contraction/mixing, or equality of the latency interval endpoints.

For the original training family, these conclusions additionally require
H_entry: Reach_train(1116; X0(theta)) subset Q(theta). The current dossier has
not discharged that application premise; see TRAINING_ENTRY_REVIEW_EN.txt.

Proof of fixed latency and exact word preservation

Call an active port influential when it can arrive before the certified first
firing, and masked when its earliest arrival is strictly after the latest
firing bound. These classes were checked uniformly over Q. There are 525
influential active ports, 15 masked active ports and 108 silent ports.

For every influential port, the ERROR phase begins after the main firing and
ends before reopening. Its gain therefore never changes during a future wave.
During idle time the port has no ERROR current, so the gain does not change
then either. Silent gains are also constant. Masked gains may change; their
raw arrivals always occur after the first firing and end before reopening, so
they do not influence any future head threshold. Recovery b may change but
acts only in protection; it does not enter the READY head equation.

Let G denote the vector of these fixed influential gains. At each permitted
source pulse all active main charges are exactly r and all active auxiliary
charges are exactly rm. No residual incoming or outgoing current survives.

Induct on network layers. The active source has latency zero. Suppose every
active parent of a cell has fixed latency d_i. Relative to a new source time
s, its influential arrivals are at s+d_i. Up to the first threshold, the cell's
charge is

  q_s(s+u) = r + sum_i G_i h_+(u-d_i),

where h_+(v)=0 for v<=0 and h(v) for 0<v<1. The same formula holds for every
future wave because G is fixed, all heads start at r, and no influential gain
changes before firing. Masked ports are absent before the threshold. The
certificate supplies an increasing first crossing before any raw pulse ends.
Its latency is therefore the same uniquely determined root d_(n,j) on each
wave, regardless of s, later gaps, masked gain history or b history.

This proves F_(n,j),k=s_k+d_(n,j), and completes the layer induction. Subtracting
two consecutive firing times gives exact equality of the inter-event gaps.
There is no rounding tolerance in this symbolic equality: numerical intervals
certify the inequalities needed to justify the formula, rather than approximate
the equality itself.

The statement includes the first future response because Q requires exact r
for active main charges. A merely nearby READY charge would add a homogeneous
term and would not justify the same exact first-wave translation argument.
