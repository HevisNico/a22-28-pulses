ADDENDUM TO proof/FUTURE_INVARIANCE_PROOF_EN.txt: GENERAL RELATIVE WIDTH EPS
============================================================================

proof/FUTURE_INVARIANCE_PROOF_EN.txt is kept byte-identical (its hash is
cited). This addendum states the two changes that make it valid for a
relative parameter half-width eps other than 1e-4, and records the values
certified at eps = 1e-3 for the campaign of this folder.

1. Section A, definition of Q(theta): "within the relative +/-1e-4
   parameter ranges" becomes "within the relative +/-eps parameter ranges".
2. Section B: "each eta is independently in [-1e-4,1e-4]" becomes "each eta
   is independently in [-eps,eps]".

No other step of the argument uses the value of eps. Every eps-dependent
quantity it uses (the bracket of M, the durations DM, DS, DE, the rates LM,
LS, LE, the cap C = 4(1 +/- eps), and the work bound W) is recomputed from
eps by both checkers:

  sweep/v23/eps_1em3/future/certify_future_eps.py           (third argument eps)
  sweep/v23/eps_1em3/future/verify_future_certificate_eps.py (fourth argument eps)

At eps = 1e-4 on the published terminal states, both reproduce the published
outputs exactly (sweep/v23/eps_1em3/future/check_v33_eps1e-4.json and
independent_check_v33_eps1e-4.json).

The theorem then holds at eps for a terminal domain Q exactly when both
checkers pass on its extracted enclosures. Its numerical constants become
those of the certificate.
