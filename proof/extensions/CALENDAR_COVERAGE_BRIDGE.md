## Transfer of the training-coverage argument

The initial-section construction is unchanged: the source at zero has the same
canonical shape and there is no hidden prior pulse. The original fifteen
initial-section inequalities are recomputed at physical epsilon 1e-3. They
establish the same starting invariants before subsequent external events.

The source is an exact finite prefix of the original schedule with unchanged
spacing 8. Sections 4–9 of the learning-coverage proof preserve a physical
witness at each successful local flow, root, reset, event-order branch and
history join. Their induction is over the events actually present, not over
a required number of 128 source pulses. Removing the later source pulses does
not authorize deleting their earlier consequences: the new queue is generated
afresh from the 28 pulses and all parent histories.

For the earlier terminal cut, the queue still contains every raw and auxiliary
boundary generated by the supplied parent emissions, plus the terminal end
event. The terminal-identity guard requires the end event to be last in the
planned queue. This test alone does not prove that an uncertain event cluster
cannot extend beyond 230. The passive terminal audit additionally requires
every retained terminal time to denote exactly 230, with zero uncertain time
terms; a joined state ending after the cut or with uncertain terminal time
cannot pass that audit. The cell loop must consume its complete queue, resolve
required internal transitions, and terminate with main and auxiliary READY
modes and no active raw input. Any unsupported branching or failed bound
prevents qualification. Thus earlier termination is an outcome to be checked,
not an assumption that 14 time units after the final source pulse suffice.

The terminal main charge and all actual learning histories are retained. Silent
auxiliary relaxation is re-evaluated at the new cut, so the wider residual
intervals at 230 are not replaced by their much smaller 1116 counterparts.
There is no state reset and no transfer of gains from another campaign.

The layer induction of Section 10 still uses the same authenticated parent
time and parameter definitions in all children. The new passive audit checks
all 216 payloads, receipts, layer verifications, parent chains and retained
time-atom definitions. These authentication checks support the binding of
the source-level proof to this run; they do not replace the local soundness
argument or reconstruct discarded intermediate expressions.

If every cell and layer completes and the above transfer is accepted under
A1–A4, then `Reach_28(230) ⊆ S28`, where S28 is the exact extracted enclosure.
No inference of this entry statement is made from the prior sampled sweep.

## Terminal inclusion and future closure

Both original directed-arithmetic future checkers are run unchanged on S28.
Their formulas are relative to future source pulses and do not require the
training cut to be 1116. The general-epsilon future theorem applies if the
finite strict inequalities pass. The new domain is Q28, constructed from
this run's terminal gain, b and silent-charge bounds, with the standard
extension of masked gains to physical caps and b to 1.1.

Successful checks would therefore connect the whole-box training enclosure
to exact preservation of future pulse intervals on the established active
mask, for arbitrary canonical future source sequences with gaps at least 20,
under the stated manual proof assumptions. Q28 is not silently identified
with the numerical domains Q32 or Q128. All unsuccessful checks must remain
in the evidence and prevent that conclusion if they occur.
