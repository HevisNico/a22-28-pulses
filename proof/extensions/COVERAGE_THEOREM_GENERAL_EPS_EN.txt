ADDENDUM TO THE LEARNING-COVERAGE THEOREM: GENERAL RELATIVE WIDTH EPS
=====================================================================

proof/learning_justification/output/LEARNING_COVERAGE_THEOREM_EN.txt ("the
theorem") is kept byte-identical (its hash is cited). It is written for the
relative parameter half-width 1e-4. This addendum lists every step that uses
that value, and records what replaces it at eps = 1e-3 for program v3.3 + C7.
No other step uses the value of eps.

1. Section 1, the family Theta: "in their prescribed relative 1e-4
   intervals" becomes "in their prescribed relative eps intervals". The
   initial perturbations stay 1e-5.

2. Sections 5.4 and 7, initial section and auxiliary cycles. These rest on
   the finite inequalities of proof/final_entry_audit/certify_initial_section.py,
   which fixes the parameter factor to [.9999, 1.0001].
   certify_initial_section_eps.py (this folder) is the same script with the
   factor [1 - eps, 1 + eps]; at eps = 1e-4 its output is byte-identical to
   proof/final_entry_audit/output/initial_section_certificate.json.
   At eps = 1e-3 all fifteen inequalities hold
   (terminal/initial_section_eps1e-3.json):

                                         eps = 1e-4           eps = 1e-3
     initial section end U0              8.6570e-5            8.6648e-5
     first auxiliary delay D1            [0.019997, 0.020092] [0.019979, 0.020110]
     work bound W (Section 7)            < 240.566579         < 240.780830
     every auxiliary cycle ends before   1.3                  1.3

   The bound W < 500 used in Section 7 therefore holds with the new value.

3. Section 8(vi), the recorded commutations. The section proves the
   commutation macro (affine/commutation.py, unchanged since v3.3) only for
   groups made of a single mark_off, with two orders and zero fire-time
   widening, and says that a general claim for raw arrivals "is not needed
   for this campaign theorem". At eps = 1e-4 all 202 recorded commutations are
   of that kind (proof/evidence/commutation_usage.json). At eps = 1e-3 they are
   not. audit_commutations.py, run on the campaign outputs, gives
   (terminal/commutation_usage_eps1e-3.json):

     group                              count   orders   widening
     raw_on, mark_on, mark_off          202     4        > 0 (kappa ~ 0.917)
     raw_off                              2     2        0
     error_on                             2     2        0

   all in layer 18, lanes 4, 5, 7 and 8. Lemma E below proves the macro in
   general, and covers these 206. Neither the no-fire cover nor the
   recovery-group fallback is used at either eps.

   LEMMA E. Fire against a group of exterior events, in READY
   ----------------------------------------------------------
   The argument is the one written in the docstring of affine/commutation.py,
   checked here against _resolve and _fire_root. Formal version of E1, E2 and
   the enclosure: proof/lean/CommutationE.lean (Lean 4, Mathlib, no sorry);
   there h(u) <= A u is proved for every u >= 0, without the concavity
   condition below.

   Setting. A live READY state s at time t0 with pulse memory; a group of
   exterior events whose order relative to the READY fire is not decided.
   Each event is raw_on (an input arrival), raw_off, or an auxiliary action
   (mark_on, mark_off, error_on, error_off); any other action makes the macro
   decline, and the caller then raises the original refusal. Q0(t) is the
   charge given by the memory without the group's arrivals, and f0 its first
   crossing of 1. All statements are for one fixed physical realization.

   E0 (events that do not move the charge). In READY, a raw_off at origin+1
   leaves Q unchanged, because the memory term of that input already stops
   its admission at origin+1 (the macro checks that the term is in memory and
   that the date is exactly origin+1). An auxiliary action changes only flags,
   b and z histories; Q does not depend on them, since pulse amplitudes are
   fixed at admission, and b and z act on q only through later admissions and
   protection. Hence, before the fire, Q = Q0 + sum over the arrivals j already
   admitted of g_j h(t - a_j).

   E1 (fire bracket). Each added term is >= 0, so Q >= Q0 on [t0, F], where F
   is the actual first threshold time. Q0(f0) = 1 gives F <= f0. On
   [t0, min(a_min, f0)) there is no arrival yet, so Q = Q0 < 1 there, and
   F >= min(a_min, f0).

   E2 (tightening). h is concave on [0, 2 ln(A/K)/(A-K)], which contains [0, 1]
   (the macro asserts 2 ln(A/K)/(A-K) > 1; it is about 2.30), and h(0) = 0,
   h'(0) = A, so h(u) <= A u on [0, 1]. The macro requires
   w >= (f0 - a_min)^+ for every realization and w < 1, so every admitted
   arrival has age < 1 before f0. Let sigma be a lower bound of Q0' on
   [a_min, f0], valid for every realization (the macro takes the derivative
   enclosure on [max(0, min a.lo), r0.hi], which contains that window), and
   G >= sum_j g_j over the arrivals (the macro takes the largest sum of upper
   gains over all orders). For t in [a_min, f0], by the mean-value theorem,

     Q(t) <= 1 - sigma (f0 - t) + G A (t - a_min),

   which is < 1 for t < (sigma f0 + G A a_min)/(sigma + G A). If sigma > 0,
   F >= f0 - kappa (f0 - a_min)^+ with kappa = G A/(sigma + G A) in (0, 1];
   otherwise kappa = 1 and E1 gives the same bound. So the emitted date
   t0 + r0 + [-kappa w, 0], with r0 an enclosure of f0 - t0 from the ordinary
   root path (or the first-crossing cover of Section 8(vi)), contains F.

   E3 (every order is kept). _fire_root certifies Q0(t0 + hi) >= 1 with hi
   below the horizon, so the fire occurs in the window, before the next
   pending event; the macro widens the window when the fire enclosure can
   reach the next event, and declines at the end of the fast section. For
   every admissible order of the group (Section 8, no equality pruned) and
   every position k of the fire in it, the macro runs the unchanged kernel
   maps: the k events before the fire, the fire reset at the common enclosure
   above, the remaining events under protection, and the flow to a common
   clock U after all of them. The actual realization follows one of these
   (order, k) branches exactly, with its own b and z histories, and an
   arrival after the fire is not admitted, as in the model. The branches are
   joined by join_states after the macro asserts that they agree on mode,
   auxiliary phases, fire object and raw flags; memory is None after the
   fire reset, so Section 9 applies as for the mark_off case.

   Hence every successful commutation, of any recorded kind, covers the actual
   realization. The 206 commutations at eps = 1e-3 are instances: 202 with an
   arrival (E1 to E3 with kappa < 1), and 4 with no arrival (w = 0, no
   widening, E0, E1 and E3).

Clarification of Section 5.1. The sentence on the exp(0) = 1 shortcut means:
the program returns exp = 1 exactly only when its argument is the exact point
0 (affine_iv.I.exp, plain branch), that is at elapsed time zero, where the
live-state fact q0 < 1 excludes a root. An argument that is symbolic, even with
center 0, goes through the mean-value form, whose center is expanded to
adjacent numbers and is therefore strict. At a positive actual elapsed time the
argument is never the exact point 0, so the open-image argument applies.
